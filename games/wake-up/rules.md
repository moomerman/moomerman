# Wake Up and Win — Rules

A fast word game. On each turn a player gets a random **letter** and a **card** of
subject prompts. They have **30 seconds** to name something matching each subject
that **starts with the letter**. 10 points per correct answer.

## Setup
- Add each player (or team) name. Scores are tracked per player.
- The game holds a single pool of **subject prompts** (e.g. "An item of clothing",
  "A male singer", "A room in the house"). Each turn deals 10 random prompts.

## A turn
1. The active player gets a **random letter** (the digital spinner).
2. A fresh hand of **10 prompts** is dealt and shown one at a time.
3. Start the **30-second timer**.
4. For each prompt, the player says an answer **beginning with the letter**.
   - Valid answer → mark correct, advance to the next prompt.
   - Stuck → **pass** to the next prompt (can't come back).
5. When time runs out, score **10 points per correct answer**.

## Rounds & winning
- Play **3 rounds** — every player takes 3 turns.
- Highest total score wins (the "$2,000 Wake Up and Win" card).
- Lowest score gets the "Slippers" card.
- Ties: tied players each take one extra turn; repeat until broken.

## Digital adaptation notes
- **Spinner** → tap to spin, lands on a random letter (Q/X/Y/Z left out so kids
  aren't stuck on near-impossible letters).
- **Prompts** → all prompts live in one shuffled pool; each turn deals 10 unique
  ones, cycling through the whole pool before any repeat.
- **Scoring** → tap **Correct** (+10) or **Pass** (skip, no points) to advance;
  can't go back. App auto-totals per player and per round.
- **Host optional** — the app shows prompts and runs the timer, so it works with a
  host reading aloud and verifying, or solo by passing the phone around.
